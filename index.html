    What does Cognimorph do?

    Cognimorph is a digital agency that delivers four connected services: digital growth (performance marketing across paid social, search and programmatic, SEO and AEO, social media management), digital build (websites, apps and software), creative production (video, design, CGI and AI video) and AI automation and training.

    Where is Cognimorph based, and who do you work with?

    Cognimorph is built by an expert team in Kathmandu, Nepal, and is accessible globally. Our main client base is in Dubai, Singapore, the United Kingdom, Australia and the United States, and we work in our clients’ time zones.

    How much does a project cost?

    We quote each project individually and price by the value we add, not a rate card. Most engagements begin with a free consultation and a fixed scope. Growth and maintenance work usually runs as a monthly retainer.

    Do you offer a free consultation?

    Yes. Tell us about your business and what you need, and a specialist will give you honest advice on the right next step, with no strings attached.

    Which advertising platforms do you manage?

    Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, X, Snapchat, Pinterest and Spotify, plus programmatic buying through StackAdapt, all with full-funnel tracking.

    How long does a project take?

    A marketing website typically takes four to six weeks, and an app build three to five months. Growth campaigns usually go live within two to three weeks of kickoff and then improve continuously.

    How do I start a project with Cognimorph?

    Send an enquiry through the contact page or email hello@cognimorph.co. We reply within one business day and arrange a call to understand your goals before proposing an approach.

    Have something worth building?

    Share the goal and the numbers. Within one business day you get a clear first plan, not a brochure.
