Digital Growth & Performance Marketing

The challenge

Strong product, growing revenue, but spend was scaling faster than orders. Meta and Google were bought separately with no shared view of what a customer actually cost.

  • Seven overlapping campaigns competing in the same auction
  • No server-side tracking, so roughly a third of conversions went unattributed
  • Creative refreshed twice a year, well past fatigue

What we did

  • Rebuilt account structure into three consolidated campaigns by intent stage
  • Deployed GA4 with Meta CAPI and server-side tagging for clean attribution
  • Moved to a fortnightly creative cycle: 6–8 new variants tested per sprint
  • Built a Looker Studio dashboard the founder checks daily
  • Introduced a post-purchase flow to lift repeat orders

What changed

Within two quarters the account was spending 18% more but returning substantially better economics. The gain came mostly from attribution accuracy and creative volume, not from a bigger budget.

  • Payback period shortened from 74 days to 41
  • Attribution gap closed from ~33% to under 8%
Measurement: GA4, Meta CAPI, Shopify · Baseline: 90 days pre-engagement

The challenge

Dispatch ran across three chat groups and a spreadsheet. Drivers were called individually, jobs were double-assigned, and nobody could answer "where is my delivery" without a phone call.

  • No single source of truth for job status
  • Manual assignment created a bottleneck at one dispatcher
  • Customer enquiries handled entirely by phone

What we did

  • Built a dispatch web app and driver mobile app on one shared backend
  • Automatic job assignment by proximity, load and driver availability
  • Live tracking with a customer-facing status link
  • Offline-tolerant sync for low-connectivity routes
  • Retained for maintenance, monitoring and quarterly releases

What changed

The dispatcher bottleneck disappeared. The same team now moves roughly a third more jobs per day, and the support line stopped being the tracking system.

  • Inbound "where is it" calls down by an estimated 70%
  • Dispatcher role shifted from assignment to exception handling
Stack: React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, React Native · Baseline: manual process audit, month 0

The challenge

A new roastery line was launching in three weeks with no video assets, no shoot booked and a modest budget that could not absorb a second production day.

  • Single production day available
  • Assets needed for four platforms at three aspect ratios
  • No existing brand film to cut from

What we did

  • Storyboarded for multi-format capture before the shoot, not after
  • One production day covering hero film, cutdowns and stills
  • Edited 1 hero film plus 13 short-form variants in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9
  • Colour graded in DaVinci for a consistent look across every placement

What changed

Planning for reuse at storyboard stage meant one shoot day produced a full quarter of content. The best-performing cut was a 12-second variant that was not in the original brief.

  • Content calendar covered for 11 weeks from one shoot
  • Top variant outperformed the hero film by 2.7×
Production: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve · Baseline: brand 6-month engagement average

The challenge

Loan documents were reviewed and keyed by hand. Every month-end produced a backlog, and the error rate rose exactly when volume peaked.

  • Each application touched by three people before approval
  • Data re-keyed between two systems with no validation
  • Compliance checks done from a printed checklist

What we did

  • Automated document intake, extraction and validation via n8n workflows
  • Rule-based compliance checks with every exception routed to a human reviewer
  • Two-way sync between the intake system and the core CRM
  • Trained the operations team to build and amend their own workflows

What changed

Straightforward applications now pass through with a single human check; complex ones get the attention they always needed. The team owns the workflows rather than depending on us.

  • Ops team has since built four further automations unaided
  • Every automated decision remains auditable and reversible
Stack: n8n, Airtable, custom API integrations · Human review retained on all approvals
