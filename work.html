A few of the products and brands we work with. Each one started with a founder’s vision; our job is to help it reach the people it was built for.

AI productivity app

Risespace

A self-development and productivity app with an AI coach built in.

Risespace brings everything a person needs to grow into one calm daily space, so habits, goals and focus reinforce each other instead of living in five different apps.

  • Habit tracker with streaks and gentle reminders
  • Tasks and goal setting that connect daily work to bigger ambitions
  • Guided meditation for focus and recovery
  • An AI coach that plans, nudges and reflects with you
Career intelligence

Hello5.ai

Career intelligence that takes you from CV to the right job.

Hello5 turns job hunting into a guided path: understand where you stand, close the right skill gaps, and apply where you are genuinely a strong fit.

  • AI CV scan with clear, actionable feedback
  • Matching to the roles that actually fit your profile
  • Skill-gap guidance on what to learn next
  • Auto-apply to matched jobs, so no opportunity slips
Fashion · natural thread

Bilva

Fashion design in natural thread, good for the body and the soul.

  • Timeless silhouettes over fast-moving trends
  • Considered, slow craftsmanship in every piece
  • Comfort that feels as good as it looks
